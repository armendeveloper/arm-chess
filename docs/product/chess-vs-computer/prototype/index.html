Chess vs Computer: prototype

Phone frame: 360 x 640 dp with 3-button navigation, the tightest size in the spec (section 4.1). Use the ◁ button for system back.

Scripted. Moves are simplified (no castling, en passant or draw rules), and the computer is a tiny mate-in-one / capture picker that "thinks" for 1.2 s. Pieces are Unicode stand-ins; the app uses the Cburnett set.

Scenarios

Promotion endgame: tap the pawn on e7, then e8. Queen or Rook mates. Bishop or Knight lets the computer mate you with Rb1.

Previews



Keyboard

Tab into the board, then use the arrows to move, Enter to select or move, Esc to deselect or close, and Ctrl+Z to undo.
